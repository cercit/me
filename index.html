My journey

Why I'm moving into product.

At the branch, I worked on loan files and customer follow-up. In working capital finance, I helped roll out a lending product. Today, I use enquiry and market data to understand what's happening across the retail business.

Those roles made me interested in how the process itself is designed: what information someone needs, where a file gets stuck, and what would help them take the next step.

I'm learning product management alongside my job. cercit is one way I'm applying it: a loan-review demo where a credit officer can see the documents, policy checks and reasons behind a recommendation.
