About me

I joined Sundaram Finance in 2014 at a branch counter in Shahpur. Twelve years later I'm on the National Retail Team in Chennai. Every role taught me the same thing — most operational failures aren't people problems, they're design problems. I kept moving by picking up the issues my previous role couldn't reach.

Day to day, two things. I track enquiry lifecycles across the branch network — where leads come in, where they stall, what automation can unstick them (Power Automate Desktop and Python). And I pull Vahan registration data alongside OEM production plans from portals like Maruti Enterprise to figure out where the market is actually going, not where the last report said it was. The Excel models I build off that data are designed to force a decision, not sit in someone's inbox. Making them work means keeping corporate, regional sales, and the dealer network pointed at the same number.

The question that changed how I think about work: I stopped asking "why did this branch miss?" and started asking "why is this process built this way, and who actually benefits from it?" That's the product management lens, as far as I can tell. I've been adding SQL and Power BI to push the analysis further.
