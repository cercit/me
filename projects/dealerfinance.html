DealerFinance is an ongoing team project with Aditya Sharan for the FinStack hackathon. We are designing a tool for dealer finance coordinators who need to choose a lender and follow a customer's loan file.

My contribution

I worked on lending rules, the customer journey, wireframes and handover. Aditya Sharan owns the PRD and sprint plan.

01

The dealer's problem

A dealer may work with several lenders. When one rejects a customer, the coordinator has to choose another, resend information and chase the status.

I mapped a proposed journey beginning with the car enquiry and consent. It checks lender fit, asks for required information and sends exceptions to a review queue.

02

A ranking decision we changed

An early version gave priority to lenders offering the dealer a trade advance. We changed that order to put customer fit, customer preference and EMI first.

Commission, lender speed and available trade advance remain visible as tags and tie-breakers. A coordinator choosing below the top fit must record why.

03

The work so far

I prepared the lending decisions, low-fidelity screens, wireflow and handover. The wireflow shows who owns each step, what information it needs and where a case can exit.

Aditya owns the PRD and sprint plan. These documents are our working handover for the platform build.

04

What remains

The project is unfinished. We still need to complete the core enquiry-to-send journey and test cases where consent is missing, credit data is unavailable or manual review is required.

The bureau-access arrangement also needs resolution before using real customer data. The completed platform build is not yet the evidence shown here.
