About me

I joined Sundaram Finance in 2014 at a branch counter in Shahpur. Twelve years later I'm on the National Retail Team in Chennai. Every role taught me the same thing — most operational failures aren't people problems, they're design problems.

Day to day, I track enquiry lifecycles across the branch network and pull Vahan registration data to figure out where the market is actually moving. The Excel models I build off that data are meant to force a decision, not sit in someone's inbox.

At some point I stopped asking "why did this branch miss?" and started asking "why is this process built this way?" That's the product lens. The IIT Roorkee program is making it formal.

My career

Four roles, all at Sundaram Finance. Each one moved me closer to the system-level view. Start with the current role, or click through the rest.

Deputy Manager, Auto National Retail Team @ Sundaram Finance

April 2024 to present · Chennai

  • Operations: Own the national lead lifecycle — first enquiry to disbursal. Pull Vahan registration data to track where OEM market share is shifting and flag gaps between what branches are doing and what the national target needs.
  • Tooling: Took scattered automation scripts from different teams, consolidated them into standard workflows. Cut 20-25% off manual processing time.
  • Dashboards: Built leading-indicator views in Power BI and Excel to track field officer productivity. The point isn't reporting — it's making sure a stuck process gets attention before month-end.

Deputy Manager, Working Capital Finance @ Sundaram Finance

April 2022 to March 2024 · Bellary

  • Bulk Diesel Finance: Set up a new lending product from scratch — no existing customers, no playbook. Beat quarterly budget targets by 10-40% once it was running.
  • Channel building: Trained branch staff and oil company officials who'd never sold financial products before, turned them into active channel partners. Portfolio grew to ₹10-11 Crore per month.

Branch Accountant @ Sundaram Finance

August 2015 to March 2022 · Bijapur · Promoted from Cashier in 2017

  • COVID workaround: When lockdowns shut down field work, built a remote KYC process over WhatsApp video so loan originations could keep going. Not elegant, but it worked.
  • Digital payments: Moved cash-paying borrowers onto digital repayment channels. Adoption went up 30-40%.
  • NPA recovery: Ran GECL restructurings for borrowers whose vehicles were sitting idle. Held customer education camps at the branch to bring delinquent accounts back before they defaulted.

Customer Support Executive @ Sundaram Finance

August 2014 to August 2015 · Shahpur

  • Loan processing, credit assessments, compliance audits. First job — learned how every step in the lending chain actually works before I started trying to fix any of them.
