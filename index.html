About me

Twelve years at Sundaram Finance, starting as a Customer Support Executive in Shahpur and now with the National Auto Retail Team at Head Office in Chennai. I didn't plan this path — every posting handed me a problem the last one hadn't.

The work itself: track how enquiries move through the system end to end, find the stalls, and fix them across branches in India, not just one. I read Vahan registration data to catch market-share shifts before they show up in a branch report, and build dashboards senior management can act on instead of just look at.

Somewhere in this, the question changed — not "why did this branch miss last month" but "why is this process shaped this way, and who does it actually serve." That's the pull toward product. Here are a few tools I work with regularly:

  • Power Automate Desktop
  • MySQL
  • Python
  • Playwright
  • Advanced Excel
