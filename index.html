01. About

Twelve years at Sundaram Finance, starting as a Customer Support Executive in Shahpur and now with the National Auto Retail Team at Head Office in Chennai. I didn't plan this path. Every posting handed me a problem the last one hadn't.

The work itself: track how enquiries move through the system end to end, find the stalls, fix them across branches in India, not just one. I read Vahan registration data to catch market share shifts before they show up in a branch report. I build dashboards senior management can act on instead of just look at. And I stay close to Associates and Field Officers on the ground, because that's the only way to know if a number is telling the truth.

Somewhere in this, the question changed. Not “why did this branch miss last month” but “why is this process shaped this way, and who does it actually serve.” That's product thinking, whatever you call it. I've been moving toward it on purpose — an Expression of Interest for the Corporate Strategy team, and the day job itself: own a process, measure what it does, redesign it when it stops working.

Branch Accountant — expanded scope through COVID @ Sundaram Finance

Mar 2020 – Mar 2022 · Bijapur, Karnataka

Same title, different job for two years. In-person Field Investigation, a hard requirement before disbursal, became impossible under lockdown. I ran FI and customer verification over WhatsApp video calls instead, timed to the 5–6 AM to 10 AM window when movement was actually possible. Four vehicles got financed and delivered through that workaround alone — two cars, one LCV, one FB-HCV.

The harder problem was borrowers whose commercial vehicles just sat idle — not running, no income, EMIs piling up, none of it their fault. Repossession would have solved our balance sheet and ruined the customer. So it wasn't the default move. We used GECL — the government's guaranteed emergency credit line for existing MSME and business borrowers — to get people through it, restructured what needed restructuring, and ran an on-branch camp once the worst had passed so customers actually knew the facility existed. Recovered a fair number of NPA accounts this way, and most customers kept their end of the restructured terms. Genuinely one of the better stretches of work I've done, for reasons that have nothing to do with the numbers.

Customer Support Executive @ Sundaram Finance

Aug 2014 – Aug 2015 · Shahpur

First job out of campus placement at KLE IMSR, Hubli. Assessed customer creditworthiness, coordinated with compliance and internal audit, ran loan processing end to end. Shaped how I've treated process rigour ever since — a design requirement, not a box to tick.
