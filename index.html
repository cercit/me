01 About

I've spent close to twelve years at Sundaram Finance, starting in a customer support role in Shahpur and now sitting with the National Auto Retail Team at Head Office in Chennai. Every posting added a layer I didn't expect.

What I actually do: track how enquiries move through the system, find where they stall, and fix that at scale, across India. I read Vahan data to catch market share shifts before they show up in branch reports, build dashboards that give senior management something to act on rather than just look at, and stay close to Associates and Field Officers on the ground — which keeps me honest about what the numbers are really saying.

Somewhere along the way I got pulled toward a different kind of question. Not "why did this branch underperform last month" but "why is this process designed this way, and who does it actually serve." That's the pull toward Product Management: the intersection of data, user behaviour, and systems thinking. I've been building toward it deliberately — through an Expression of Interest for the Corporate Strategy team, and through the work I already do: owning a process end-to-end, measuring its outcomes, and redesigning it when it breaks.
