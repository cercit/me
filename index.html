About me

Twelve years at Sundaram Finance, from the frontlines in Shahpur to the National Retail Team in Chennai, taught me that operational scaling is fundamentally a product problem. I advanced by actively seeking out the systemic friction my previous roles couldn't solve.

The work itself spans execution and strategy. On the execution side, I track end-to-end enquiry lifecycles and deploy automated workflow fixes across the national branch network using Power Automate Desktop and Python. On the strategy side, I synthesize Vahan data and OEM intelligence, leveraging platforms like the Maruti Enterprise portal, to map production plans across the consumer car and commercial cab markets. I build advanced Excel models that force management action, and I drive the alignment between our corporate leadership, regional sales forces, and external dealer networks to hit those targets.

Over time, the core question shifted for me. It went from "why did this branch miss its target?" to "why is this system designed this way, and who does it actually serve?" That shift from localized troubleshooting to systemic, cross-functional design defines my approach to product management. To support this, I continuously expand my analytical stack, integrating SQL and Power BI for deeper data modeling.
